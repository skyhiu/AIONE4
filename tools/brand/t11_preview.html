AIONE logo and Boot Up proposals

Pick a logo, then press Play to see and hear its 5-second Boot Up together. Every sound hit is a visual event, built from the sound's measured beat times. Made by the implementer for 4.0.0-alpha.4; not in the app yet (T1.2).

Beat = where the hit is loudest (5 ms steps), measured on the hit's own track after the same echo and level as the finished sound. The animation's event for each hit is placed at that time and driven by the audio clock on every frame, so it lands within one frame (about 17 ms at 60 fps).

Menu sounds on Home (proposed mapping)

"Hold right" plays a held direction as AIONE does: the press, then a repeat every 90 ms after 400 ms, with the move sound on at most every 180 ms and 3 dB softer. Menu sounds play at AIONE's starting menu level (60 %).

Sounds: "50 Menu Interface SFX" by ColorAlpha, CC BY 4.0 (coloralpha.itch.io/50-menu-interface-sfx); the Boot Up sounds are adaptations (pitched, layered, echoed, levelled). Lettering: M PLUS Rounded 1c (SIL OFL 1.1), as outlines.
